# Store listing texts

Copy these into the Google Play Console (*Grow → Store presence → Main store listing*) and App Store Connect (*your app → the version → App Information / Version Information*).
Add **English** as the default language and **German** as a translation in both stores. Character limits are noted; every text below fits.

## Google Play

### English (en-US) – default

**App name** (max 30)
```
Super Wortschatz: German Words
```

**Short description** (max 80)
```
Learn German vocabulary A1–C2: daily words, your own sentences, smart reviews.
```

**Full description** (max 4000)
```
Learn the German words you actually need – a few every day, with your own sentences, and reviews that make them stick.

Super Wortschatz has over 7,000 carefully chosen words from A1 to C2. Each one comes with its article (colour-coded der / die / das), plural or verb forms, an English meaning, a natural example sentence and pronunciation.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your level – A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 or C2, or combine several.
• Choose how many new words you want per day (1–30).
• Write your own sentence with every word. The app checks it instantly – did you use the word (in any form: leidet, litt, gelitten), capitals, punctuation – and shows grammar tips.
• Review with spaced repetition: words come back just before you'd forget them, as meaning questions, German from English, article quizzes and fill-in-the-gap.
• Watch your progress: words per level, mastered words, streak and a searchable list of everything you've learned.

OPTIONAL AI FEEDBACK
Want real corrections of grammar, word order and word choice? Connect your own API key from Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google) and get an explanation and another example for every sentence. Google's Gemini usually has a free daily quota. Your key stays on your phone and your sentences go directly to the provider you chose.

MADE FOR LEARNERS
• Interface in English or German
• Works fully offline
• Daily reminder at a time you choose
• Dark mode
• Backup and restore – move your progress between phones

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Super Wortschatz is ideal for preparing for Goethe, telc or TestDaF exams, for integration courses, for work in Germany, Austria or Switzerland – or simply for anyone who wants a bigger German vocabulary.
```
